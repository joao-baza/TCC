# Third-party notices

This foundation does not redistribute third-party assets. It records the
approved source inventory and immutable source revisions in
`pid/catalog/sources.json` so later catalog ingestion can preserve provenance.

The Draw.io inventory entry references private authorization held by the
project. That private authorization must not be represented as a public license.
Every future redistributed asset must carry its own source, license, attribution,
checksum, and derivation metadata in a validated catalog manifest.
